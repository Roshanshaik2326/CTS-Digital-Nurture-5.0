# Observer Pattern

## Definition

Observer is a Behavioral Design Pattern that establishes a one-to-many dependency between objects. When the subject changes, all observers are automatically notified.

---

## Components

- Subject
- Observer
- Concrete Subject
- Concrete Observer

---

## Advantages

- Loose coupling
- Automatic notifications
- Easy to add new observers
- Follows Open/Closed Principle

---

## Disadvantages

- Notifications may impact performance if there are many observers.
- Debugging can become harder with many subscribers.

---
